# Short football lessons 6–7

Both lessons reuse the teacher lesson engine, flashcards, pairs, first-attempt goals, error review and champion finish. Each has 12 required exercises, six possible goals, three teacher-reviewed original sentences and independent lesson/task/storage IDs.

## Football 6: actions

Four ready-made third-person phrases: pasa el balón, chuta, marca un gol, para el balón. Teach ¿Qué hace…? without a conjugation table. Repeat ¿Quién…?, el balón and un gol. Passing, shooting, a scored goal and a saved ball use separate scenes. The question identifies the red player when another player is visible. Short and full answers are listed explicitly; an unrelated action is rejected.

## Football 7: feelings

Four states: contento/contenta, triste, cansado/cansada, nervioso/nerviosa. Repeat está/están and adjective agreement; teach ¿Cómo está…? for a current state. Male footballer and female goalkeeper portraits establish agreement. Two copies represent plural people. Sad, tired and nervous have distinct facial/body cues; current-state questions use estar consistently.

## Localization and assets

All new instructions, translations and feedback are supplied in Russian, Ukrainian and English. Spanish is the target language; catalog titles also have Spanish entries. Existing lessons, task IDs and user results are preserved.

Eight original illustrations were generated with built-in imagegen, then resized and compressed to WebP. Final paths and exact generation prompts are recorded in football67-illustration-prompts.json. No external image license or third-party logo is required.
